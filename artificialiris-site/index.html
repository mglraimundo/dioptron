Methods and interpretation

01 · Passive sulcus

HumanOptics recommends the smallest estimated sulcus diameter. Its practical horizontal WTW rule uses +0.5 mm, rounded down to the next half-millimetre.

floor[2 × (WTW_caliper + 0.5)] / 2

A parallel Hashemian regression estimates horizontal STS. Agreement is not proof of fit, as both depend on WTW.

02 · Sutured sulcus

The 2026 manufacturer IFU advises a diameter at least 1 mm smaller than the sulcus. This planner floors the derived STS minus 1.0 mm to a 0.5 mm trephine.

floor[2 × (STS_est − 1.0)] / 2

This is a rule applied to an imperfect STS estimate, not an independently validated sutured sizing equation.

03 · Capsular bag

Vass et al. derived an AL/K regression (70 eyes with CTR, R² ≈ 0.315). Crawford et al. used measured bag diameter minus 0.5 mm. Combining the two is exploratory.

Bag = 3.44 − 0.056K + 0.713AL − 0.0135AL²

Use the evacuated bag after CTR placement to confirm. Typical adult ArtificialIris diameter is around 10 mm.

04 · IOLMaster 700 adjustment

There is no validated IOLMaster 700 → STS conversion for ArtificialIris. In one 107-eye study, IOLMaster 700 WTW averaged 12.14 mm versus 11.45 mm manually.

Caliper proxy = WTW_700 − 0.69 mm

This is a between-device mean offset, not an individual conversion. Outputs using it are clearly labelled exploratory. Enter an actual caliper WTW when possible.

Primary literature & manufacturer instructions

  1. HumanOptics ARTIFICIALIRIS IFU (2026), sections 11–12. Passive sulcus: estimated smallest sulcus diameter; sutured: ≥1 mm undersized; bag: usually 10 mm.
  2. HumanOptics ArtificialIris Sizing Guide (2017). Koch WTW +0.5 mm rule and routine trephine ranges.
  3. Hashemian et al., Journal of Current Ophthalmology (2018). STS = 0.796 + 0.973 × caliper WTW; high-myopia cohort; 95% limits of agreement approximately ±0.55 mm.
  4. Ang et al., Eye and Vision (2022). IOLMaster 700 versus manual caliper WTW differ and are not interchangeable.
  5. Vass et al., JCRS (1999). Capsular-bag diameter regression; R² = 0.3145.
  6. Crawford et al., Clinical & Experimental Ophthalmology (2022). Endocapsular ArtificialIris, bag size minus 0.5 mm.
  7. Reinstein et al., J Refract Surg (2009). WTW-based prediction of STS weak (95% prediction interval approximately ±1.11 mm).
